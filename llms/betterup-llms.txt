# BetterUp

> BetterUp is an enterprise Human Transformation Platform combining human and AI-powered coaching, grounded in behavioral science, to help organizations develop leaders, managers, and workforces. It serves 750+ enterprise customers with a network of 4,000+ coaches across 70+ countries. BetterUp exposes no public developer API; integration with the platform is delivered through packaged enterprise connectors (Workday, Salesforce, Slack, Microsoft Viva, SAP SuccessFactors, Degreed) and HRIS/SSO provisioning.
